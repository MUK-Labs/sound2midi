Listen to your Mac’s audio

Install BlackHole 2ch. In Audio MIDI Setup, create a Multi-Output Device with BlackHole and your speakers. Use it as the Mac’s sound output, then select BlackHole below.

Send MIDI to Max or a DAW

In Audio MIDI Setup, open MIDI Studio and enable the IAC Driver. Choose its bus here as MIDI output, and in your music software as MIDI input. Route synthesized sound directly to your speakers to avoid feeding it back into the detector.
